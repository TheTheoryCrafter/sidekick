M3 · Interaction All demos

  1. Hover over the cat with the mouse. It may get shy (10 s cooldown, 70% chance).
  2. Bring the cursor near it from a distance. It looks at you or walks over to you.
  3. Click it to startle it, or double-click it to make it dance. On a phone, tap it.
  4. Drag it: it hangs from your pointer. Let go gently and it drops and lands.
  5. Throw it: it flies, bounces off the walls and lands grumpy.
  6. On a phone, swipe anywhere except on the cat: the page must still scroll.
