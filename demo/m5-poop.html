Poop

Now and then the cat squats and leaves a pile behind it (on its tail side). Click a pile to clear it. It may cheer when you do. Piles stay put when you scroll: on a card they stick to the card, on the floor they stick to the page. At most 5 at once; each one disappears after 10 minutes (or 20 s with the link above).

It never poops over anything clickable. Tick "links along the bottom" to fill the floor with links: the cat stops pooping on the floor, and "poop now" plays the animation but leaves nothing behind.
